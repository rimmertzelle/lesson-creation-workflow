# Wired or wireless: what really differs?

Same goal — getting data across — with very different rules.

</div>

<div class="compare">
<div>

## Ethernet (wired)

- **Medium:** own cable per device
- **Turn-taking:** hardly needed (switch)
- **Speed under load:** stays stable
- **Weak spot:** cables, fixed location

</div>
<div>

## Wi-Fi (wireless)

- **Medium:** one shared channel
- **Turn-taking:** listen, wait, retry
- **Speed under load:** drops with users
- **Weak spot:** interference, crowding

</div>
</div>

<div class="row cols-2">
<div class="card">

### Choose wired when…

latency and stable throughput matter: servers, gaming, video calls.

</div>
<div class="card orange">

### Choose wireless when…

mobility matters more than peak performance.

</div>
</div>

<div class="takeaway">

## Key insight

The difference is not the speed on the box — it is **whether you share the medium**.
