# Student Personas — HBO-ICT, HZ University of Applied Sciences

These three archetypes represent the range of students typically found in the HBO-ICT bachelor programme. Use them to calibrate lesson difficulty, exercise design, and instructional tone.

---

## Persona 1 — Lars (HAVO instroom)

**Age:** 18
**Background:** Straight from HAVO. Has no professional work experience yet.

**Strengths:**

- Used to an academic-style learning environment with structured guidance
- Reasonably strong in abstract thinking and written tasks
- Comfortable with theory when it is well-explained

**Challenges:**

- Finds it hard to see why something matters until it is explicitly connected to a real-world case
- Can struggle with open-ended problems where there is no single right answer
- Limited practical experience makes it hard to judge when a solution is "good enough"
- Cognitive load builds quickly when theory, tools, and context are introduced simultaneously

**Motivation:** Wants to do well academically, but intrinsic drive depends heavily on whether the subject feels relevant or cool.

**Design implication:** Needs clear structure, worked examples before open tasks, and explicit connections between theory and practice.

---

## Persona 2 — Fatima (MBO instroom)

**Age:** 22
**Background:** Completed an MBO-4 in ICT before entering the HBO programme. Has worked part-time in a helpdesk or junior IT role.

**Strengths:**

- Strong practical intuition — has seen real problems and knows what "working" looks like
- Higher inner drive; chose this path deliberately and takes ownership of her learning
- Comfortable with tools, procedures, and working under pressure
- Life experience gives her a mature perspective in group work and professional contexts

**Challenges:**

- Can underestimate the value of theoretical foundations ("I already know how to do this")
- Abstract or conceptual assignments without a practical anchor feel frustrating or pointless
- May need to build academic writing and argumentation skills
- Sometimes overcautious about being "wrong" in front of younger classmates

**Motivation:** Wants to grow beyond execution into design and decision-making. Responds well when autonomy and prior experience are respected.

**Design implication:** Anchor theory in practice first. Acknowledge her experience explicitly. Use tasks that reward depth over speed.

---

## Persona 3 — Daan (International / non-traditional instroom)

**Age:** 24
**Background:** Has a non-linear path — gap year, self-taught programming, possibly international background or a switch from another field.

**Strengths:**

- High curiosity and self-direction; has learned a lot independently
- Comfortable with ambiguity and experimentation
- Often strong in one or two areas (e.g., coding, design) with genuine passion

**Challenges:**

- Uneven knowledge base — strong in some areas, with surprising gaps in others
- May resist structured learning if it feels like it slows him down
- Group collaboration can be uneven: takes over or checks out depending on the task
- May not yet have developed consistent work habits or planning skills

**Motivation:** Driven by mastery and interesting problems. Needs to be challenged — routine tasks without a creative or complex dimension lose him quickly.

**Design implication:** Offer extension challenges. Use open problems that reward initiative. Make room for different entry points into the same task.
